--- INTRO ---
01  Narrator (UZ): Truck stopga keldingiz. Yoqilgʻi quyish, dush olish va tunash uchun joy soʻrash kerak. Tinglang.

--- DIALOGUE ---
02  Voice EN 1: Hi. I need fuel on pump twelve.
      UZ: Salom. Oʻn ikkinchi kolonkada yoqilgʻi kerak.
03  Voice EN 2: How much do you want, or fill it up?
      UZ: Qancha quyay, yoki toʻldiraymi?
04  Voice EN 1: Fill it up, please. Diesel and DEF.
      UZ: Toʻldiring, iltimos. Dizel va DEF.
      (spoken as: Fill it up, please. Diesel and D.E.F..)
05  Voice EN 2: Do you have a rewards card?
      UZ: Bonus kartangiz bormi?
06  Voice EN 1: Yes, here it is. Can I get a shower too?
      UZ: Ha, mana. Dush ham olsam boʻladimi?
07  Voice EN 2: Sure. You're number five. I'll text you when it's ready.
      UZ: Albatta. Siz beshinchisiz. Tayyor boʻlsa, SMS yuboraman.
08  Voice EN 1: Do you have reserved parking for tonight?
      UZ: Bugun tunga band qilinadigan joy bormi?
09  Voice EN 2: Yes, it's fifteen dollars. Spot forty-two, in the back.
      UZ: Ha, oʻn besh dollar. Orqa tomonda, qirq ikkinchi joy.

--- RECAP ("Takrorlang": played twice with a pause) ---

      UZ: Toʻldiring, iltimos.
