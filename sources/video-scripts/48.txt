--- DIALOGUE ---
02  Voice EN 2: What brings you in today?
      UZ: Bugun nima bilan keldingiz?
03  Voice EN 1: I have a fever and a bad sore throat.
      UZ: Isitmam bor va tomogʻim qattiq ogʻriyapti.
04  Voice EN 2: How long have you had these symptoms?
      UZ: Bu belgilar qachondan beri bor?
05  Voice EN 1: Since yesterday. I'm a truck driver. Can I still drive?
      UZ: Kechadan beri. Men trak haydovchisiman. Haydasam boʻladimi?

      UZ: Bu dori uyqu keltirishi mumkin. Ichgandan keyin haydamang.
07  Voice EN 1: Do you have something that doesn't cause drowsiness?
      UZ: Uyqu keltirmaydigan dori bormi?
08  Voice EN 2: Yes, the non-drowsy version. Take one tablet every six hours.
      UZ: Ha, uyqu keltirmaydigan turi bor. Har olti soatda bitta tabletka iching.
09  Voice EN 1: Thank you very much.
      UZ: Katta rahmat.
